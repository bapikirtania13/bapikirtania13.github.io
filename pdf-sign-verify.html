🔍 About PDF Sign Verify

In today's digital era, official documents such as e-Aadhaar, income tax filings, utility bills, educational certificates, and employment contracts are digitally signed rather than handwritten. When viewed on standard mobile PDF viewers, these documents often display a confusing yellow question mark (?) or state "Signature Not Verified."

PDF Sign Verify - Green Tick (developed by Kirtania under parent company Pito Store™) bridges this crucial gap on mobile devices. It provides an enterprise-grade cryptographic validation engine that parses standard CMS (Cryptographic Message Syntax) and PKCS#7 digital signature containers embedded inside PDF documents according to the official ISO 32000 specification.

100% Privacy by Design: Zero Document Uploads

Unlike online web converters or cloud portals that require you to upload your sensitive identity documents to an unknown server, PDF Sign Verify executes all cryptographic hashing, certificate parsing, and signature verification strictly on your device's processor. Your documents never leave your storage.

📖 How to Use PDF Sign Verify (Step-by-Step)

Follow these quick steps to verify any digitally signed PDF document in seconds:

1

Select & Open Your PDF Document

Launch the application and tap "Select PDF". You can choose any PDF stored in your internal storage, Downloads folder, SD card, or directly import a file from WhatsApp, Google Drive, Gmail, or your file manager using the system document picker.

💡 Tip: Password-protected PDFs are supported; the app will prompt for the document password locally.
2

Automatic Instant Cryptographic Analysis

Once selected, the built-in parser automatically locates the PDF's signature dictionaries, extracts the signed ByteRange, and recalculates the SHA-256 or SHA-1 cryptographic message digest to confirm that no alterations have occurred since the document was signed.

⚡ Execution: Verification completes in under 500 milliseconds for standard documents.
3

Inspect Signer & Certificate Information

The app presents a comprehensive verification summary displaying:

  • Signer Common Name (CN): Legal identity or entity that signed the file.
  • Signing Timestamp: Exact date, time, and timezone when the signature was sealed.
  • Document Integrity: Confirms whether the content was modified after signing.
  • Certificate Authority (CA) Chain: Root CA, Intermediate CA, and issuing details.
  • Validity Period: Certificate start and expiry dates.
4

View the Green Tick & Export Verification Report

If the signature is intact and valid, the certified Green Tick confirmation is displayed. You can also export or share a detailed cryptographic audit report for legal, corporate, or archival records.

✅ Verification complete: Validated according to international digital signature standards.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Answers to common questions regarding PDF digital signature verification on Android.

Q: Does PDF Sign Verify upload my Aadhaar card or PDFs to the internet?

No, absolutely not. PDF Sign Verify is architected from the ground up as a purely local on-device utility. The document never leaves your phone's memory. All cryptographic hash calculations and certificate validation take place directly on your phone's CPU.

Q: Why do Adobe Reader or mobile viewers show a yellow question mark?

Most standard mobile PDF viewers do not include local cryptographic certificate trust stores or lack automated verification engines for regional Certifying Authorities (such as India's CCA). Because the viewer hasn't verified the certificate chain, it shows an unverified warning. PDF Sign Verify inspects the actual cryptographic signature data to establish true document authenticity.

Q: Can I verify password-protected PDF files?

Yes. If your document (such as an e-Aadhaar or bank statement) is encrypted with a password, the app will securely prompt you to unlock it on your device so the cryptographic signature stream can be extracted and verified.

Q: What is the difference between a scanned image signature and a digital signature?

A scanned signature is merely an image pasted on top of a page and can be easily forged or copied. A true digital signature is mathematical cryptography: it seals the document with a private cryptographic key and creates a tamper-evident ByteRange hash. Any modification after signing breaks the signature mathematically.
