Our Story & Engineering Philosophy

In an era dominated by predatory subscription apps, intrusive cloud telemetry, and bloated interfaces, finding straightforward tools that simply work without harvesting user data has become rare.

Pito Store™ was founded with a singular conviction: software should serve the individual, not the advertising broker. Whether building on-device cryptographic verification engines for official identity documents, realistic financial market simulators to protect retail traders from real capital loss, or distraction-free note capture utilities, every line of code is written with native speed, zero bloat, and total privacy in mind.

All our published applications run smoothly on modern and legacy Android systems alike, avoiding unnecessary server hops and keeping sensitive user operations strictly sandboxed on the user's hardware.

Core Tenets

The Three Pillars of Our Work

Every application published by Kirtania adheres strictly to these principles.

🔒

1. 100% On-Device Processing

When you verify a digital signature on an e-Aadhaar card or save personal journal notes, your data is never shipped to remote cloud servers. Calculations take place locally on your phone's processor.

⚡

2. High-Performance Native Code

Built using native Android technologies (Kotlin, Jetpack, Flutter), our apps achieve sub-100 millisecond cold starts, rock-solid 60/120fps UI rendering, and minimal battery consumption.

🛡️

3. Zero Deceptive Patterns

No fake countdown timers, no forced subscriptions, no bait-and-switch paywalls, and no requesting unnecessary permissions like Contacts, Camera, or precise GPS location.

🛠️ Technical Specializations

Applied Cryptography

CMS (RFC 5652), PKCS#7 (RFC 2315), X.509 certificate chains, SHA-256 byte-range digest extraction, and ISO 32000 PDF signature dictionaries.

Market Simulation Engines

Android Architecture
