Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 18, 2026

1. Introduction

Kirtania ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting the privacy of users ("you" or "your") who use our mobile applications. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our Android applications available on Google Play, including PDF Sign Verify - Green Tick, Trade Test - Pro Paper Trading, Crypto Test - Pro Paper Trading, Pito Notes, and any future published titles.

By using our applications, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree with the terms of this Privacy Policy, please do not use our applications.

2. Special Provision: PDF Sign Verify (On-Device Local Processing)

For our digital signature verification application, PDF Sign Verify - Green Tick:

  • Zero Document Uploads: When you select a PDF file to verify signatures, all cryptographic operations (including PKCS#7 / CMS signature extraction, ByteRange integrity hashing, and X.509 certificate chain validation) are performed strictly locally on your device.
  • No Cloud Storage: Your PDF documents, signature metadata, certificate data, and verification reports are never sent to our servers, stored in the cloud, or shared with any third party.
  • No Account Required: You do not need an account, login, or registration to verify documents.

3. Information We Collect

Across our other applications, we may collect the following limited types of information:

  • Information You Provide: Data you voluntarily enter into our applications, such as notes in Pito Notes, simulation paper trade orders and watchlists in Trade Test or Crypto Test, or user preference settings.
  • Usage Data: Anonymized interaction statistics, including features accessed, session frequency, and interface preferences to help optimize stability.
  • Device Diagnostics: Device model, operating system version, crash stack traces, and performance metrics collected via Google Play Console or Firebase Crashlytics to resolve bugs.

4. How Information Is Used

We use collected data solely for legitimate operational purposes:

  • To provide, maintain, and enhance the performance of our applications.
  • To diagnose application crashes, resolve bugs, and ensure cross-device stability.
  • To protect users against security risks and fraudulent activities.
  • We do NOT sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties.

5. Permissions

Our applications request only the minimum Android permissions essential for core functionality:

  • Storage / Media: To read user-selected PDF files for on-device signature verification or to store and retrieve personal notes.
  • Internet Access: To fetch market quotes for paper trading simulators, verify root certificate revocation lists (if enabled), or display essential developer updates.

6. Third-Party Services

Where applicable, applications may interface with trusted third-party developer frameworks:

  • Google Play Services: For secure app distribution, licensing, and updates.
  • Firebase Crashlytics / Analytics: For aggregate crash diagnostics and stability tracking.
  • Google AdMob: For privacy-compliant in-app advertisements where applicable.

7. Data Security & Children's Privacy

We adopt industry-standard security safeguards. Our applications are not directed towards children under 13, and we do not knowingly solicit personal data from children.
