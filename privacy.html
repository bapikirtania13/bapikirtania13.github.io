Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 22, 2026 | Effective Date: October 22, 2026

Welcome to Kirtania ("we," "our," or "us"). We operate the website https://bapikirtania13.github.io/ and publish mobile applications for Android on the Google Play Store, including PDF Sign Verify - Green Tick, Trade Test - Pro Paper Trading, Crypto Test - Pro Paper Trading, Pito Notes – Smart Notes & AI, and related software tools.

Your privacy is our utmost priority. This Privacy Policy details our practices concerning the collection, use, maintenance, disclosure, and protection of information when you browse our website or use any of our mobile applications.

1. Fundamental Privacy Philosophy: On-Device Architecture

We believe that software should be built with privacy by design. Wherever technically feasible, our applications process your files, documents, and inputs strictly locally on your device. We do not operate remote servers that harvest your confidential files or read your personal writings.

2. Application-Specific Data Privacy Framework

A. PDF Sign Verify - Green Tick

  • Zero Document Uploads: When you open an e-Aadhaar, PAN card, invoice, tax return, or certificate for signature validation, the entire verification algorithm (CMS/PKCS#7 signature extraction, SHA-256 byte-range digest hashing, and X.509 certificate chain validation) is executed entirely on your Android device's processor.
  • No Remote Storage or Telemetry: PDF contents, document names, personal identifiers, and verification certificates are never uploaded to any remote server or third party.
  • No Account Required: You can verify documents without registering, providing an email, or logging into an account.

B. Trade Test - Pro Paper Trading

  • Virtual Paper Trading Only: All orders, cash balances, and portfolios are 100% simulated with virtual currency.
  • No Financial Credentials: The application never requests, collects, or stores bank account numbers, UPI IDs, Demat credentials, or broker API tokens.
  • Network Use: Internet access is utilized solely to retrieve live public market quote feeds.

C. Crypto Test - Pro Paper Trading

  • Virtual Cryptocurrency Simulation: Market orders, limit orders, and portfolios operate with demo virtual USDT.
  • No Private Keys or Wallets: The app never asks for blockchain wallet seed phrases, private keys, or exchange login credentials.
  • Network Use: Internet connectivity is used strictly to stream real-time cryptocurrency price information from public market indices.

D. Pito Notes – Smart Notes & AI

  • Local SQLite Storage: All notes, checklists, tags, and drafts are saved directly in your phone's sandboxed local SQLite/Room database.
  • Zero Unauthorized Cloud Sync: Your personal writings are never sent to external cloud servers unless you explicitly choose to export or share a note using your phone's system share menu.

4. Information Automatically Collected (Log Files & Web Analytics)

Like most modern websites, our hosting servers automatically record standard log entries when you navigate our site. These log entries may include:

  • Internet Protocol (IP) addresses (anonymized where applicable)
  • Browser type and version
  • Referring and exit web pages
  • Operating system and device platform

These log entries are not linked to any personally identifiable information and are used solely for website maintenance, security auditing, and performance optimization.

5. Mobile Application Permissions Explained

Our Android applications request only the minimum Android runtime permissions essential for their advertised functions:

  • Storage / Media Access (READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE / Document Picker): Used in PDF Sign Verify strictly to access the specific PDF document you designate, or in Pito Notes to export backup files to your storage upon your command.
  • Internet Access (android.permission.INTERNET): Used in Trade Test and Crypto Test to download real-time market quote data, and on our website to deliver web pages, style sheets, and Google AdSense advertisements.
  • Zero Sensitive Permissions: None of our applications request access to your contacts, call logs, SMS, camera, microphone, or precise GPS location.
