🪙 About Crypto Test

Cryptocurrency markets trade 24/7/365 with unprecedented volatility. For beginners and experienced traders alike, testing strategies directly on live exchanges with real money can lead to sudden, severe capital losses due to flash crashes, liquidation wicks, and emotional trading.

Crypto Test - Pro Paper Trading (developed by Kirtania under parent company Pito Store) provides a completely secure sandbox. You trade against live market prices using virtual demo USDT. You never have to connect an exchange API key, risk private wallet keys, or complete KYC verification.

⚠️

Educational Simulator Notice

Crypto Test is an educational paper trading simulation. Virtual currencies (simulated USDT, BTC, ETH) inside this app have no real-world monetary value, cannot be redeemed for fiat or actual crypto, and cannot be withdrawn. This app does not provide financial or cryptocurrency investment advice.

📖 How to Use Crypto Test (Step-by-Step)

Follow these simple steps to start simulating crypto trades immediately:

1

Start with Your Virtual Sandbox Balance

Open Crypto Test to automatically receive an allocation of simulated virtual USDT. There is no signup wall, wallet setup, or credit card prompt. You can immediately begin exploring the crypto market interface.

💡 Tip: Use your virtual balance to test realistic trade sizes like 2% or 5% per position.
2

Browse Market Trends & Top Gainers

Explore the comprehensive crypto market board sorted by market capitalization, 24-hour top gainers, top losers, and highest trading volume. Filter across major pairs like BTC/USDT, ETH/USDT, SOL/USDT, and high-momentum altcoins.

3

Inspect Dynamic Candlestick Charts

Select any trading pair to launch responsive candlestick charts. Switch between timeframes (1m, 5m, 15m, 1h, 4h, 1D), analyze support and resistance levels, and examine volume trends before entering a position.

⚡ Real-time updates: Live ticking prices refreshed continuously.
4

Place Simulated Buy or Sell Orders

Tap Trade to execute a simulated order:

  • Market Order: Fill instantly at the current best available simulated price.
  • Limit Order: Set an exact entry price and wait for market price action to reach your trigger.
  • Stop Orders: Protect your simulated capital by configuring automated stop-loss exits.
5

Track Your Virtual Portfolio Breakdown

View your asset allocation pie chart in the Portfolio tab. Track how much of your capital is in virtual USDT vs held in active crypto positions. Monitor unrealized profit/loss update with every market tick.

6

Analyze Historical Trades & Refine Strategies

Review your completed trades history to see your winning percentage, average holding time, and profit factors. Test dollar-cost averaging (DCA), swing trading, or breakout trading strategies over time without risking a single dollar.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Common questions answered regarding crypto paper trading.

Q: Do I need a crypto exchange account or wallet to use Crypto Test?

No. Crypto Test is a standalone simulator. You do not need accounts on Binance, Coinbase, or any exchange, nor do you need MetaMask or hardware wallets. You get virtual USDT immediately upon installing.

Q: Are the cryptocurrency prices real?

Yes. The app feeds live market pricing data from leading global cryptocurrency price indexes so that your paper trading reflects authentic market conditions and price action.

Q: Can I withdraw my virtual cryptocurrency profits?

No. All balances, coins, and profits within Crypto Test are strictly virtual and educational. They have zero cash value and cannot be transferred outside the simulation environment.

Q: Is Crypto Test suitable for testing bot and DCA strategies?

Yes. Many users practice recurring dollar-cost averaging (DCA), support/resistance breakouts, and swing-trading strategies to measure profitability before risking capital in real markets.
