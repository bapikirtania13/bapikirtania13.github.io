✅ Google Play Live 🛡️ Cryptography & Digital Signatures

PDF Sign Verify - Green Tick

A dedicated cryptographic PDF digital signature verification application for Android. Designed to validate authentic digital signatures on documents such as e-Aadhaar, PAN cards, digital certificates, invoices, and contracts. It parses embedded CMS/PKCS#7 signature structures, verifies document ByteRange integrity, validates X.509 certificate chains against root authorities, and resolves unknown question marks into verified green ticks — completely offline with zero file uploads.

✓ Real CMS/PKCS#7 Verification ✓ Document Hash & ByteRange Check ✓ X.509 Certificate Chain Validation ✓ e-Aadhaar & Form 16 Compatible ✓ 100% On-Device & Private
