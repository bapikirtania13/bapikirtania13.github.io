How to Verify Digital Signatures on e-Aadhaar and PAN Cards on Android

✍️ Kirtania 📅 Published: November 3, 2026 ⏱️ 6 min read 🏷️ Practical Tutorial

When you download an official electronic document from UIDAI (e-Aadhaar) or the Income Tax Department (e-PAN), you will notice a signature box at the bottom. On desktop computers running specialized software, this displays an authentic green tick mark. But when you open that same PDF on your Android smartphone, it almost invariably displays an unverified yellow question mark (?) with the warning: "Validity Unknown" or "Signature Not Verified".

Many government offices, passport centers, bank KYC verifiers, and universities reject printouts or electronic submissions that still show the question mark. In this step-by-step guide, you will learn why this occurs and how to verify digital signatures directly on Android with complete data privacy.

1. Why Does the Yellow Question Mark Appear?

The yellow question mark does not mean your e-Aadhaar or PAN card is fake or expired. It simply means your phone's current PDF viewer is unable to cryptographically validate the signature.

When an authority like UIDAI generates an e-Aadhaar, it signs the document using a cryptographic certificate issued by India's Controller of Certifying Authorities (CCA). To verify this signature, software must:

  • Extract the PKCS#7 digital signature container from the PDF structure.
  • Inspect the signer's public key certificate.
  • Traverse the certificate hierarchy to confirm that the certificate was signed by a recognized root authority (e.g., CCA India, e-Mudhra, NSDL, or CDAC).
  • Recalculate the SHA-256 byte-range digest across the document to prove no bytes were modified.

Standard Android PDF viewers (such as Google Drive Viewer, WhatsApp built-in viewers, or default gallery tools) simply do not contain this cryptographic PKI validation engine. Thus, they display a question mark by default.

2. The Severe Privacy Risks of Online Signature Converters

Many users turn to online web tools promising to "convert your yellow question mark to a green tick online". This is an extremely dangerous practice.

⚠️

Warning: Never Upload Identity Documents to Cloud Portals

Your e-Aadhaar and PAN card contain sensitive personal identification data: your full name, date of birth, residential address, biometric reference IDs, and facial photograph. Uploading this file to unknown third-party cloud servers risks identity theft, financial fraud, and unauthorized data brokers harvesting your personal information.

5. Understanding the Cryptographic Audit Certificate

Once verified, the app allows you to view and export an official Verification Report. This report summarizes the cryptographic proof:

Verification Field Meaning
Common Name (CN) The legal entity or individual that applied the private key signature.
Certifying Authority (CA) The licensed body (e.g. CCA, e-Mudhra) that confirmed the signer's identity.
Digest Algorithm The mathematical hash used (typically SHA-256 with RSA encryption).
ByteRange Integrity Confirms that zero bytes were changed after the signature was affixed.

6. Common Troubleshooting & FAQs

Q: What if the signature is invalid or forged?

If someone opened the PDF and edited any number or letter in your Aadhaar card or PAN card, the SHA-256 byte-range digest will not match the decrypted hash. The app will immediately alert you: "Document modified after signing — Signature Invalid!".

Q: Does the app need internet access to verify files?

No. PDF Sign Verify is pre-packaged with trusted Root CA certificates and performs all cryptographic calculations on your phone's CPU. You can verify documents while completely disconnected from Wi-Fi or mobile data.
