💡 About Trade Test

Over 90% of retail traders lose capital in stock and derivative markets due to a lack of disciplined strategy testing and emotional control. Jumping straight into live markets with real money is expensive.

Trade Test - Pro Paper Trading (developed by Kirtania under parent company Pito Store™) solves this by offering a zero-risk sandbox where traders can test technical setups, practice price-action trading, experiment with options strategies (Calls, Puts, Straddles, Iron Condors), and build discipline before committing real capital to live brokerage accounts.

⚠️

Educational Simulator Notice

Trade Test is strictly a virtual simulation and educational utility. All trading balances, orders, profits, and losses are simulated with virtual currency. The application does not process real money deposits, withdrawals, or live brokerage orders, and does not provide financial or investment advice.

📖 How to Use Trade Test (Step-by-Step)

Follow this structured guide to begin paper trading and refining your trading playbook:

1

Initialize Your Virtual Trading Account

Open the app and configure your virtual portfolio. You start with a realistic demo balance to simulate authentic risk sizing and portfolio allocation. No bank connection or Demat account is needed.

💡 Tip: Treat your virtual capital as if it were real money to develop healthy risk habits.
2

Search & Build Your Custom Watchlist

Search for your favorite stocks, benchmark indices (such as Nifty 50, Bank Nifty), or sector tickers. Add them to your personalized watchlist to monitor live price fluctuations and daily percentage changes.

⚡ Fast search: Find any equity or index contract instantly.
3

Analyze Market Depth & Option Chains

Tap on any asset to inspect interactive charts, bid-ask spreads, and option chain matrices. View strike prices, Call/Put Open Interest (OI), change in OI, and implied volatility to plan high-probability entry points.

4

Execute Simulated Paper Trade Orders

Tap Buy or Sell to open the virtual order panel. Configure:

  • Order Type: Market Order (instant execution) or Limit Order (target specific price).
  • Position Sizing: Set quantity or lot size according to your risk-per-trade rule.
  • Risk Controls: Set strict Stop-Loss (SL) and Target (TP) price levels.
5

Track Open Positions & Real-Time P&L

Monitor your active trades in the Positions tab. Watch unrealized Profit & Loss update in real-time. Adjust stop-losses, trail profits, or manually square off positions whenever your profit target is reached.

📊 Live analytics: Real-time P&L calculation matching live tick moves.
6

Review Trade Journal & Performance Statistics

Every completed paper trade is logged in your history. Review your overall win rate, average risk-to-reward ratio, largest winning trade, and drawdown metrics to continuously improve your trading strategy.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Everything you need to know about paper trading with Trade Test.

Q: Do I need to link a bank account or Demat account to use Trade Test?

No, never. Trade Test is an independent educational simulation. It never asks for bank details, Demat login, PAN, or financial credentials. You get instant virtual demo funds right after installing.

Q: Can I withdraw profits earned inside the app?

No. All profits, losses, and balances within Trade Test are virtual and for simulation purposes only. They possess zero real-world monetary value and cannot be transferred or withdrawn.

Q: How does paper trading help me become a better trader?

Paper trading allows you to test trading rules, price patterns, and risk-management strategies over hundreds of market cycles without the anxiety of losing real savings. It helps you build emotional discipline and ironclad trading rules.

Q: Can I reset my virtual balance if I run out of funds?

Yes. You can easily reset your portfolio balance from the settings menu to restart your simulated trading experiments fresh at any time.
